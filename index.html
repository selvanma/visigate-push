Zero Missed Alerts

WhatsApp Meta Cloud API + Automated MacroDroid Phone Rings

Most community apps fail because residents keep notifications muted or forget to open the app. VisiGate Alerts solves this with a dual-channel dispatch pipeline:

  • 💬
    Meta Cloud WhatsApp API: Sends an instant interactive WhatsApp message with live [Approve Entry] and [Deny Entry] buttons directly to the resident's WhatsApp.
  • 🔔
    Automated MacroDroid Phone Rings: Simultaneously fires an automated webhook via the Master Router to ring the resident's phone with a custom alert tone, guaranteeing immediate attention.
  • ⚡
    Sub-Second Gate Sync: Once the resident clicks Approve on WhatsApp, the Guard App at the gate lights up green in under 1 second.
